Bleep It! — Chrome Web Store Listing
=====================================

SHORT DESCRIPTION (132 chars max):
Automatically censors offensive language on any webpage. Blur, replace with symbols, or swap in silly words. Perfect for families.

─────────────────────────────────────

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:

Bleep It! scans every webpage you visit and instantly censors profanity, slurs, and other offensive language — keeping your browsing experience clean and family-friendly.

━━━ HOW IT WORKS ━━━

Once installed, Bleep It! runs silently in the background. It detects offensive words in page text and applies your chosen censoring method:

• Blur Mode — Words are visually blurred out. Hover to get a hint, or right-click to temporarily reveal.
• Symbol Mode — Offensive words are replaced with #@$!% characters.
• Silly Words Mode — Swap profanity with random harmless words like "flurple" or "snickafribble" for a lighter experience.

━━━ KEY FEATURES ━━━

✓ Works on dynamic content — Handles infinite scroll, pagination, and single-page apps. New content is censored the moment it loads.
✓ Custom word list — Add your own words to censor via right-click or the options page. Your list syncs across all your devices via your Google account.
✓ Right-click reveal — Need to see what was censored? Right-click any blurred word to temporarily reveal it for a few seconds.
✓ Right-click remove — Accidentally bleeped a word? Right-click and remove it from your custom list instantly.
✓ Three censoring modes — Choose between blur, symbols, or silly word replacement.
✓ Lightweight & fast — No jQuery, no heavy frameworks. Built with modern browser APIs for minimal performance impact.
✓ Privacy-first — All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers.

━━━ IDEAL FOR ━━━

• Parents who want a cleaner browsing experience for their children
• Anyone who prefers not to see profanity while reading articles, social media, or forums
• Workplaces and shared computers

━━━ HOW TO ADD CUSTOM WORDS ━━━

1. Highlight any word on a webpage
2. Right-click and select "Bleep this word!"
3. The word is now censored on every page you visit

You can also manage your custom dictionary from the extension's options page — add, review, or remove words at any time.
