Bleep It! Options

Options


Accidentially add a word to your custom dictionary? Reveal your added words, edit the text area with only the words you want in your custom dictionary, and click "Update User Defined Words!". This will update your custom dictionary and filter the words the next time you load a webpage. In order for this to work properly, each word as to be on a line of its own.
