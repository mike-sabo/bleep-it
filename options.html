Tips

  • Add words: Highlight any word on a page → right-click → "Bleep this word!"
  • Reveal a word: Right-click a censored word → "Reveal word" (shows for 4 seconds)
  • Remove a word: Right-click a censored word → "Remove from bleeped words"
  • Dynamic content: Bleep It! automatically handles infinite scroll, pagination, and single-page apps.
  • Sync: Your custom word list syncs across all your devices via your Google account.
